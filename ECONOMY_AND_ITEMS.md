# ECONOMY_AND_ITEMS — City of Shadows

## Money sources
- Story missions: $300–$2500 (once each).
- Races: $500 / $800 per win (repeatable).
- Deliveries: $250 per completed run (repeatable).
- Starting money: $1200.

## Rules
- Ownership is permanent and saved; duplicates are impossible (buying an owned
  car selects it instead). Mission rewards cannot be farmed: completion is
  recorded before payment sync.
- Tuning is stored per car in the save and applied to real physics
  (see VEHICLE_SYSTEM.md).
- Properties give a rest point: full heal + save.
