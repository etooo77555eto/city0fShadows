# STORY_BIBLE — City of Shadows

## Premise
Meridian Bay: a coastal city that glitters on the surface and rots politely
underneath. Adam Kane, a driver who left five years ago after a job went wrong,
returns for a quiet life. Within a day he is holding other people's secrets —
and every powerful person in the city wants them.

Themes: loyalty vs survival, debts that outlive friendships, the price of
staying honest in a city that pays for silence.

## Main characters
- **Adam Kane** — the player. A wheelman with a code: no names, no cargo he
  hasn't agreed to, no friends left behind. The story tests all three rules.
- **Marcus Reed** — Adam's oldest friend; fixer, charmer, debtor. His betrayal
  five years ago (selling Adam's route to Hale's people) is the wound the plot
  keeps reopening. The player chooses to forgive or cut him off (m08), which
  feeds the ending.
- **Lena Morgan** — investigative journalist at the Bay Ledger. Brave, dry,
  allergic to being rescued. Her document caches (m02) start the ledger arc.
  Player chooses whether Adam keeps them sealed (trust) or reads them.
- **Detective Daniel Cross** — MBPD. Building a case against the Hale Group
  off the books. Player chooses to help or refuse him (m04); helping him is one
  key to the hopeful ending.
- **Victor Hale** — CEO of the Hale Group; built the waterfront, owns the
  dealership, funds half the council. Not a cartoon villain: he genuinely
  believes order is worth any price. His offer (m07) can be refused to his face
  or pretended-accepted.
- Minor: **Rosa Vega** (garage owner), **Theo Park** (street racer).

## Decisions & endings
Tracked decisions: `lena_trust` (sealed/read), `cross_deal` (help/refuse),
`victor_deal` (pretend/refuse), `marcus_fate` (forgiven/cutoff). Relationships
are numeric per character and shown through dialogue tone data.
Endings (selected in the m10 finale):
- **Hopeful** — refused Hale + helped Cross: the raid happens at dawn.
- **Tragic** — played along with Hale or cut Marcus off: the ledger vanishes.
- **Ambiguous** — anything else: the bay keeps its secrets.
